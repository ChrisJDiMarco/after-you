A GAME OF TWO POSSIBILITIES

You offer.
They choose.

All nine pucks are shared. Amber owns two opposite corners. Blue owns the other two.

  1. Pick two pucks.Click any two on the table.
  2. Make an offer.Drag forward from the first puck. Both arrows have the same strength and opposite directions. Release to commit.
  3. Hand over the choice.Your opponent clicks one of the highlighted pucks to launch it. The other stays, unless something hits it.
  4. Watch the consequences.A pocket scores for its owner, whoever took the shot. Once everything stops, your opponent makes the next offer.

First to four wins, checked after the whole shot settles. If both reach four, the higher score wins; equal scores draw. At twelve offers each, the higher score wins. You can cancel an uncommitted drag by returning to its puck, or pressing Escape.
